Honest limits: the model knows printed-style digits 0-9 from MNIST — big, centered strokes work best. It cannot read letters, multi-digit numbers or messy cursive, and the accuracy shown is on MNIST test digits (your handwriting may differ). Training uses a 9k subset so it finishes in about a minute on a laptop; full MNIST would score higher.
